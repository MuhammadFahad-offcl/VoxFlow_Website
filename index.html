Questions owners ask first

What happens in the free audit?

A short call where we go through the tasks your team repeats every day. You get a list of what an agent can take over and a rough idea of the hours it would save. There is no obligation to continue.

My business is not a clinic or a salon. Can you still help?

Yes. If your team answers the same questions, takes bookings, confirms orders or chases follow-ups, that work can be automated. The audit is where we find out how much.

Which languages does the agent reply in?

English and Roman Urdu on WhatsApp. Voice agents speak Urdu or English.

What happens when the agent cannot help a customer?

It hands the conversation to your staff. In the Lahore clinic, the team steps in only when a patient needs a person.

Every booking is written to a Google Sheet as it happens, and your team gets a daily report.

Who looks after it once it is live?

We do. Hosting, monitoring and maintenance are part of the monthly support plan.
