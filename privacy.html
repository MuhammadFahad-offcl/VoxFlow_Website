Privacy

Your details, in plain words.

Last updated 20 November 2026

VoxFlow AI Solutions (“VoxFlow”, “we”) is run from Lahore, Pakistan. This page explains what this website collects, and what happens to personal details when we build and run an agent for a business.

What this website collects

  • The callback form, when it is shown: your name, your phone or WhatsApp number, the type of business you pick and an optional note about what takes your team the most time. It is sent to VoxFlow and used only to contact you about your audit.
  • The WhatsApp button. It opens WhatsApp with a message you can edit. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. After that, WhatsApp (Meta) handles the message under its own terms, and we see your number and what you wrote.
  • Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and a few button clicks, such as the audit button and the demo. It records event names and the business type you pick from the list. It does not record anything you type.
  • Cookies. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. The savings calculator runs on your own device and sends nothing.

When we build an agent for a business

The business that hires us decides what its agent asks customers for. We handle that information on the business’s behalf, only to run the agent and report to the team. We do not sell personal details and we do not use them for advertising.

Depending on the project, we may use these services to run an agent: WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta), AI models from OpenAI and Groq, Supabase and Google Sheets for storing bookings, Vapi for voice calls, and n8n to connect the steps. Each handles data under its own terms.

If an agent has chatted with you, the business you were talking to is your first contact for questions about your details.

How long we keep it

We keep website enquiries only as long as we need them to follow up with you. Data from a client’s agent is kept as agreed with that business, and returned or deleted when the project ends if the business asks.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp or by email and we will reply.
