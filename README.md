# VoxFlow

**Your team goes home. Your business keeps working.**

This is the website of VoxFlow AI Solutions, an AI automation agency based in Lahore, Pakistan. VoxFlow builds AI agents and automations that answer customers, take bookings and finish the repeat work, so a business saves staff hours and cost.

Website: [voxflow.agency](https://voxflow.agency)

## What you will find on the website

- **A demo you can try.** A sample WhatsApp conversation, in English or Roman Urdu, where you play the customer and book an appointment with the agent.
- **Your kind of business.** Pick your industry and see which daily tasks an agent can take over and what that saves.
- **One night with a VoxFlow agent.** A short story you scroll through: a customer messages at 2:14 AM, the agent replies and books, and the team arrives at 9:00 AM to find the work done.
- **Live work.** The WhatsApp appointment agent running in a private clinic in Lahore since October 2026.
- **A savings calculator.** Enter the hours your team spends on manual tasks and what an hour costs, in PKR or AED, to see what automation could save each month and year.
- **A free automation audit.** Book a short call to find out what is worth automating in your business.

## What VoxFlow builds

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp agents | Reply the moment a customer messages, answer from your own services and policies, then book or hand over to staff |
| Voice agents | Answer or place phone calls in Urdu or English, take the booking and log what was said |
| Booking and reminders | Confirmations, reminders, cancellations, rescheduling and no-show tracking |
| Workflow automation | Move information between WhatsApp, Google Sheets, email and other tools so nobody copies it by hand |
| Custom AI agents | Multi-step agents that plan, use your tools and finish a task, with a dashboard when you need the numbers |

## Live work

A WhatsApp receptionist for a private clinic in Lahore, live since October 2026. Patients arrive from the clinic's Facebook and Instagram ads and message at any hour. The agent answers their questions, checks the clinic's booking requirements, books the appointment, sends a token number and a reminder, and gives the team a daily report. Staff step in only when a patient needs a person.
